1. Before installing, check that existing records may be reset: the community installer uses a complete image and may clear settings and kitchen records. To preserve data on an existing compatible kitchen installation, read the GitHub v0.2.0 installation guide and use its segmented upgrade.
2. After the opening animation, short-press OK to enter. No network setup or date confirmation is needed. UP/DOWN moves the selection; short OK opens or changes it. Double UP returns to the previous page; hold UP goes directly home.
3. In Today’s Menu, choose the number of people, time and meal combination, then select the draw action. Holding OK on home uses your remembered conditions. On a result, DOWN draws again without a limit, OK opens ingredients, and holding OK toggles a favorite.
4. On ingredients, DOWN adds the dish to the shopping list, holding DOWN scrolls long lists, and OK begins the steps. In a recipe, OK advances and UP goes back a step. Short DOWN scrolls long text, then opens timers once at the bottom; hold DOWN shows ingredients.
5. On a timed step, the first OK starts its timer; the next OK advances while timing continues. From home, select the permanent Timers entry at the bottom and press OK to reopen it. One short UP from the default home selection also selects Timers.
6. In Timers, UP/DOWN selects an item, hold OK pauses/resumes, hold DOWN cancels that item, and double UP returns. Short OK acknowledges a finished timer. Interrupted running timers recover paused after a power loss and require acknowledgement. Canceling a timer does not erase recipe progress.
7. In Fridge, open a category and select ingredients, then hold OK for suggestions. Mark shopping items as bought and view favorites and cooking records in the collection. Settings includes sound, volume, excluded ingredients, preferences and difficulty.
8. Recent dishes receive a lower draw weight but can still return. Recency uses completed cooking sessions rather than calendar days. Adjust quantities and cooking according to your ingredients and equipment; recipes and illustrations remain under review.
