What is for dinner? Let NABO, your white-capped kitchen companion, help you choose. Set the number of people, available time and meal combination, then draw a home-style dish. Keep drawing until something feels right.

This v0.2.0 experience release includes 145 recipes with complete dish illustrations, 58 pantry ingredients, a shopping list, favorites and cooking records. Start cooking without setting a date. Recent dishes receive a lower draw weight based on completed cooking sessions, and your last menu stays available until you replace it or finish cooking.

Check the ingredients and follow the steps at your own pace, with up to three independent timers. A permanent Timers entry on the home menu lets you reopen, pause, resume or cancel them after leaving a recipe. The entire selected card turns warm orange so your selection is easier to see.

Everything works offline without network setup. This is an experience release: recipes and artwork remain under review, and acceptance through three actual cooking sessions has not yet been completed. The cover is promotional art; gallery captions distinguish dish illustrations, code-rendered interfaces and device frame captures.
