# Kitchen asset notices

The editable recipes, artwork, animation originals and voice provenance are maintained in the `codex/kitchen-model` branch of [Liutupi/nabo-kitchen](https://github.com/Liutupi/nabo-kitchen/tree/codex/kitchen-model). This directory contains the runtime resource packs used by the independent firmware branch.

NABO character artwork, pictures, video, audio and recipe prose are excluded from the source-code MIT license. Public source storage does not grant a separate MIT license for these materials; contact the project author for reuse, as stated in the source project's `NOTICE.md`.

The bitmap fonts derive from Source Han Sans SC Medium and Archivo Black. Their SIL Open Font License texts and source provenance are included in `licenses/`. Preserve those notices when redistributing the font assets. The firmware template retains its own license and component notices.

Resource validity and schema checks are technical checks. New recipe instructions and illustrations remain review drafts pending cooking and visual acceptance.
